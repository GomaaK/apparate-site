Experience shaped our purpose

  1. 20 years ago

Final QC follows international quality standards (AQL), agreed with every client.

Critical defects
Zero
acceptance

Safety or regulatory failures, such as a stray needle or an unsafe sharp component.

Major defects

Frequently asked questions

What does Apparate do?

Apparate is an end-to-end apparel manufacturing and quality-assurance partner based in Egypt. We manage the full journey for local and regional brands, from brief and feasibility to sampling, bulk production, quality control and delivery, through one accountable team.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order is 100 pieces per color. Final minimums are confirmed in a project-specific feasibility review, because materials, trims and construction can affect them.

How many pieces can you produce per month?

Up to 50,000 pieces per month across a network of 50+ specialized production partners. Capacity and factory allocation are confirmed for each project.

How long do sampling and bulk production take?

An initial sample takes about 5 working days and a pre-production sample about 3 working days. Standard bulk production takes about 4 weeks, and about 5 weeks in peak season. Bulk timing starts after pre-production approvals and materials are confirmed.

What quality standards do you inspect to?

Every order passes four quality gates: sample inspection, first-piece inspection, inline QC at 10–20% of production, and final inspection. Final QC follows AQL standards agreed with the client: zero acceptance for critical defects, AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects.

Which printing and embroidery techniques do you offer?

Printing: DTF, DTG, rubber, vinyl (including silver reflective and glitter), sublimation, silicone and silk screen with up to 10 colors. Embroidery: flat, 3D puff, appliqué, patches, chenille, sequins and specialty threads. Techniques can be combined in one product.

What products do you manufacture?

Sportswear, knitwear, everyday and lifestyle wear, outerwear, baby and kidswear, and scarves and textile accessories such as printed scarves and tote bags, across knitted, woven and denim fabrics.

Do you work with clients in Saudi Arabia and the GCC?

Yes. For regional clients we manage production and quality oversight in Egypt through FOB handover, with licensed partners handling export documentation, customs, freight and shipping. High-volume Saudi and GCC shipments are expected to move mainly by sea, with Sokhna and Safaga as potential ports.

How do I start a project with Apparate?

Share your product reference or tech pack, quantity and colors, fabric, printing or embroidery, accessories, target delivery date and destination. An experienced apparel account manager reviews the brief, identifies the manufacturing and sourcing route, highlights key risks and confirms the next step.
