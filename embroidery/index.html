Frequently asked questions

Which printing and embroidery techniques do you offer?

Printing: DTF, DTG, rubber, vinyl (including silver reflective and glitter), sublimation, silicone and silk screen with up to 10 colors. Embroidery: flat, 3D puff, appliqué, patches, chenille, sequins and specialty threads. Techniques can be combined in one product.
