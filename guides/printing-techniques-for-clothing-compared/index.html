Garment printing techniques compared

The techniques

What each one gives you

DTF

Direct-to-film · e.g. DRP9

Full-color artwork printed onto film and heat-transferred, sharp on cotton and blends.

DTG

Direct-to-garment

Artwork printed straight onto the garment for detailed, multi-color designs.

Rubber

Raised rubber print

A raised, tactile print that stands off the fabric surface.

Vinyl

Silver reflective & glitter · e.g. AirFit

Cut vinyl applied with heat, including silver reflective and glitter finishes.

Sublimation

All-over print & laser cut · scarves, Molped, SSC

Dye sublimation for all-over prints on polyester and satin, with laser-cut finishing. Used on scarves, Molped and SSC.

Silicone

e.g. beIN SPORTS, PAO

A soft-touch silicone print for clean logos. Used for beIN SPORTS and PAO.

Silk screen

Up to 10 colors per design · SSC & AXA tote bags

Classic screen printing with up to 10 colors per design. Used for the SSC and AXA tote bags.

We can mix more than one printing type in one product.

Frequently asked questions

Which printing and embroidery techniques do you offer?

Printing: DTF, DTG, rubber, vinyl (including silver reflective and glitter), sublimation, silicone and silk screen with up to 10 colors. Embroidery: flat, 3D puff, appliqué, patches, chenille, sequins and specialty threads. Techniques can be combined in one product.
