Sampling and bulk production timelines for apparel

Plan about 5 working days for an initial sample, about 3 for a pre-production sample, and about 4 weeks for standard bulk production (about 5 in peak season).

The working timelines

Sampling

Initial sample5approx. working days
Pre-production sample3approx. working days

Bulk production

Standard production4approx. weeks
Peak-season production5approx. weeks

Bulk-production timing begins after the required pre-production approvals and materials are confirmed.

Each timeline is confirmed after reviewing complexity, fabric and trim availability, quantity, finishing, manufacturing allocation, sample requirements, and approval timing.

Questions brands ask us

Frequently asked questions

How long do sampling and bulk production take?

An initial sample takes about 5 working days and a pre-production sample about 3 working days. Standard bulk production takes about 4 weeks, and about 5 weeks in peak season. Bulk timing starts after pre-production approvals and materials are confirmed.
