Printing techniques

Printing, mastered

Direct-to-film · e.g. DRP9

DTF

Full-color artwork printed onto film and heat-transferred, sharp on cotton and blends. Example: DRP9 · Oversized kids T-shirt.

Direct-to-garment

DTG

Artwork printed straight onto the garment for detailed, multi-color designs.

Raised rubber print

Rubber

A raised, tactile print that stands off the fabric surface.

Silver reflective & glitter · e.g. AirFit

Vinyl

Cut vinyl applied with heat, including silver reflective and glitter finishes. Example: AirFit · Performance T-shirt.

All-over print & laser cut · scarves, Molped, SSC

Sublimation

Dye sublimation for all-over prints on polyester and satin, with laser-cut finishing. Used on scarves, Molped and SSC. Example: Molped · Printed pajama set (through Doodle Factory).

e.g. beIN SPORTS, PAO

Silicone

A soft-touch silicone print for clean logos. Used for beIN SPORTS and PAO. Example: PAO · Tank top (Doodle Factory collaboration).

Up to 10 colors per design · SSC & AXA tote bags

Silk screen

Classic screen printing with up to 10 colors per design. Used for the SSC and AXA tote bags. Example: AXA · Printed tote bag.

We can mix more than one printing type in one product.

Frequently asked questions

Which printing and embroidery techniques do you offer?

Printing: DTF, DTG, rubber, vinyl (including silver reflective and glitter), sublimation, silicone and silk screen with up to 10 colors. Embroidery: flat, 3D puff, appliqué, patches, chenille, sequins and specialty threads. Techniques can be combined in one product.
